Sri Lanka Travel Knowledge Base


DESTINATIONS

Colombo:
Capital city of Sri Lanka.
Attractions:
- Galle Face Green
- National Museum
- Gangaramaya Temple

Suitable for:
City tours, shopping and food experiences.


Galle:
Coastal city famous for Galle Fort.
UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Suitable for:
History, photography and beaches.


Mirissa:
Popular beach destination.

Activities:
- Whale watching
- Surfing
- Beach relaxation


Ella:
Mountain destination.

Attractions:
- Nine Arch Bridge
- Ella Rock
- Little Adam's Peak

Suitable for:
Hiking and nature lovers.


Bentota:
Beach destination.

Activities:
- Water sports
- River safari


TRANSPORT

Train:
Scenic and affordable.
Popular route:
Kandy - Ella train.


Bus:
Low cost transport between cities.


Tuk Tuk:
Suitable for short distance travel.

Respect local culture.

Carry sunscreen and water.

Check weather before activities.
