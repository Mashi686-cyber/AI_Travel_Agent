DESTINATIONS


Colombo:

Capital city of Sri Lanka.

Attractions:
- Galle Face Green
- National Museum
- Gangaramaya Temple

Suitable for:
City tours, shopping and food experiences.



Galle:

Coastal city famous for Galle Fort.

UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Attractions:
- Galle Fort
- Beaches
- Historical buildings

Suitable for:
History, photography and beach experiences.



Mirissa:

Popular beach destination.

Activities:
- Whale watching
- Surfing
- Beach relaxation

Suitable for:
Beach lovers and adventure travelers.



Ella:

Mountain destination.

Attractions:
- Nine Arch Bridge
- Ella Rock
- Little Adam's Peak

Suitable for:
Hiking and nature lovers.



Bentota:

Beach destination.

Activities:
- Water sports
- River safari
- Beach relaxation

Suitable for:
Families and beach travelers.



==================================================
TRANSPORT INFORMATION
==================================================


Train:

Scenic and affordable transport option.

Popular route:
- Kandy to Ella train

Suitable for:
Mountain journeys and scenic travel.



Bus:

Low cost transport between cities.

Suitable for:
Budget travelers.



Tuk Tuk:

Suitable for short distance travel.

Available in tourist areas.



==================================================
FOOD INFORMATION
==================================================


Popular Sri Lankan foods:


Rice and Curry:

Traditional Sri Lankan meal with rice,
vegetables, meat or seafood curries.



Kottu Roti:

Popular street food made with chopped roti,
vegetables, eggs or meat.



Hoppers:

Traditional Sri Lankan pancake.

Types:
- Egg hoppers
- Plain hoppers



Seafood:

Popular in coastal areas.

Includes:
- Crab
- Prawns
- Fish dishes

WEATHER INFORMATION
==================================================


Sri Lanka has a tropical climate.


Average Temperature:

25°C - 35°C


Best Travel Period:

West and South Coast:
December to April


East Coast:
May to September


Hill Country:
January to April
