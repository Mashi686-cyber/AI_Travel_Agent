Destination:
Sigiriya


Overview:

Sigiriya is one of the most famous historical destinations in Sri Lanka.
It is known for the Sigiriya Rock Fortress, ancient architecture,
paintings and beautiful surroundings.
It is ideal for history, culture and adventure travelers.

- Carry enough water.
- Wear comfortable shoes.
- Respect the historical site.
