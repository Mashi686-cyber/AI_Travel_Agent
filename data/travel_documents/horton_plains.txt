Destination:
Horton Plains


Overview:

Horton Plains is a beautiful national park located in the
central highlands of Sri Lanka.
It is famous for hiking trails, mountains, forests and
the popular World's End viewpoint.
It is ideal for nature and adventure travelers.

Weather:


Climate:
Cool mountain climate.


Temperature:
10°C - 20°C


Best Season:
January to March is suitable for hiking.


Best Time To Travel:
Early morning is recommended because of clear views at World's End.



Travel Tips:


- Start hiking early morning.
- Carry a jacket because temperature is low.
- Wear comfortable hiking shoes.
- Carry water and snacks.
- Follow national park rules.
