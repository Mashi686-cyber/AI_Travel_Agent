SRI LANKAN FOOD INFORMATION


Rice and Curry:
A traditional Sri Lankan meal with rice, vegetables, meat or seafood.


Kottu Roti:
A popular street food made with chopped roti and vegetables.


Hoppers:
A bowl-shaped pancake usually eaten with curry or sambol.


String Hoppers:
Steamed rice noodle dish served with curries.


Seafood:
Popular in coastal areas such as Mirissa and Galle.


Pol Sambol:
A coconut-based spicy side dish.


Watalappan:
A traditional Sri Lankan coconut custard dessert.


Ceylon Tea:
Famous Sri Lankan tea produced in hill country regions.
