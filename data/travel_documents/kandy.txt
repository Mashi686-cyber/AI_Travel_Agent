Destination:
Kandy


Overview:

Kandy is a famous cultural city in Sri Lanka.
It is known for the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic,
beautiful mountains, lakes and traditional cultural experiences.
It is one of the best destinations for history and culture lovers.
