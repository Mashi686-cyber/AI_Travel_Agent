Destination:
Nuwara Eliya


Overview:

Nuwara Eliya is a beautiful hill country destination in Sri Lanka.
It is known as "Little England" because of its cool climate,
tea plantations, waterfalls, gardens and colonial architecture.
It is suitable for nature and family travelers.
